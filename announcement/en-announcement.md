# Announcing Iran Open Privacy (draft 0.1)

Iranian users cannot tell which digital products actually protect their data. Privacy policies are long, unversioned and unchecked. A badge without evidence is theater.

Iran Open Privacy (IOP) is an open, community-run standard and verification program:

- 47 checkable controls in 9 domains, mapped to GDPR, ISO 27701 and OWASP ASVS
- Company privacy documents hosted in a public repository, with semantic versions and change feeds
- Three levels named by what is proven: Declared (automated, never called certified), Verified (two independent community reviewers plus a maintainer, public report), Continuously Verified (paid accredited audit, not offered until a legal entity and an auditor exist)
- Everything open: code (AGPL-3.0), standard (CC BY-SA 4.0), reports (CC BY 4.0)

This is a draft. No company has been verified. We need critics, reviewers (pseudonymous participation is possible), pilot companies (Iranian startups and SaaS), and two lawyers.

Repository: https://github.com/iranopenprivacy/iran-open-privacy
Site: https://iranopenprivacy.org
Contact: contact@iranopenprivacy.org

IOP is independent of any government and of any company it verifies. It is not a regulator, not a law firm and not a security audit.
